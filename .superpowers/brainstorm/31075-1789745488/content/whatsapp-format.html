What changed vs. the old message

  • Items grouped under bold category headers with a matching emoji — scan by category instead of hunting through one long list
  • Each line drops the repeated pack-size text (e.g. "1 Box (10 pcs)") — that only matters when picking the item, not confirming an order
  • Totals get their own clearly separated block with icons (🧾 items, 💰 value) instead of being buried in a paragraph
  • Customer details get a labeled section header instead of running straight into the item list
  • A delivery note line at the bottom confirms free/TN or "charge to be confirmed" for other states
